# Student Applicant Workflow

This document details the step-by-step workflow for students applying through the **Web-based Scholarship Profiling and Reporting System**. 

As per the system architecture, applicants **do not need to create user accounts** or log in. The process is designed to be seamless, public-facing, and strictly focused on data collection and document submission.

---

## Phase 1: Accessing the Portal
1. **Navigate to the Application Page**: The student visits the public URL designated for scholarship applications.
2. **Review Guidelines**: The student reads the general instructions, eligibility criteria, and a checklist of required documentary requirements before starting the form.

---

## Phase 2: Completing the Application Profile
The student fills out the digital form, which is divided into logical sections to build a comprehensive applicant profile.

* **Part I: Personal Information** - Basic details (Name, Student ID, Course, Age, Address) and Enrollment Information (Classification like Regular/Irregular).
* **Part II & III: Household & Parent/Guardian Information** - Detailed profiling of the household head, main sources of income, government assistance (like 4Ps), and detailed educational/employment status of both parents/guardians to gauge financial need.
* **Part IV: Household Composition** - Listing all members of the household, their ages, occupations, and whether they are currently studying.
* **Part V: Academic Information** - Inputting previous semester GWA and declaring academic honors (Dean's/President's Lister).

---

## Phase 3: Special Circumstances & Document Uploads
Depending on the student's profile, they will fill out conditional sections and be prompted to upload specific supporting documents.

* **Special Categories**:
  * **Person with Disability (PWD)**: Requires PWD ID and/or Medical Certificate.
  * **Solo Parent**: Requires Valid Solo Parent ID.
  * **Indigenous Peoples (IP)**: Requires NCIP Certificate or Tribal Chieftain Certification.
  * **Sports, Dance, and Musical**: Requires proof of participation/awards.
  * **Indigent Family**: Requires Barangay Certification, DSWD-Assessed report, or Certificate of Indigency.
* **Academic Documents**: All applicants must upload a Certified Copy of Grades or a Recommendation Letter by the Dean.

*Note: The system securely captures these file uploads and links them directly to the applicant's profile for later verification by the Staff.*

---

## Phase 4: Certification and Submission
1. **Data Privacy Consent**: The student reads the Data Privacy Declaration (compliance with RA 10173).
2. **Electronic Certification**: The student checks the mandatory boxes certifying that all inputted data is true and uploaded documents are authentic.
3. **Electronic Signature**: The student provides their full name as a digital signature.
4. **Submit**: The student clicks the final submit button.

---

## Phase 5: Post-Submission
1. **Reference Number Generation**: Upon successful submission, the system generates and displays a unique **Application Reference Number**. The student must save this number to track their application status later.
2. **System Handoff**: The application is officially queued in the centralized database with a "Pending" status. 
3. **Decision Support Trigger**: In the background, the system's Decision Support component automatically cross-references the student's profile against uploaded official records (e.g., CHED, TES lists) to check for duplicate applications or existing beneficiaries, flagging the application for the Scholarship Staff to review.
